Rounded label capsule with an optional status dot — section eyebrows, categories, applied filters.

```jsx
<Tag dot="ink">Objetivo</Tag>
<Tag dot="negative">Desafios</Tag>
<Tag dot="positive">Necessidades</Tag>
<Tag tone="soft">Visão geral</Tag>
<Tag tone="accent" dot="ink">Em destaque</Tag>
```

- `soft` grey tag is the page-section eyebrow on the canvas; `white` sits on grey cards.
