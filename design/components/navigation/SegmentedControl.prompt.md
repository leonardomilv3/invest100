In-card filter switch between asset classes or views — black active pill in a hairline capsule.

```jsx
<SegmentedControl options={['Commodities', 'Ações', 'Renda fixa', 'Índices']} value={cls} onChange={setCls} />
<SegmentedControl size="lg" fullWidth options={['Comprar', 'Vender']} />
```

- 2–5 options. For time ranges use `RangeToggle`.
